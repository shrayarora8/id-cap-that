# 30-second demo script

Three lines. Say them like you're talking to someone, not reading a list —
leave about two seconds between each so the checks don't queue.

---

**1. The buzzwords** — lands in ~2 seconds, no internet needed

> "We're going to revolutionise the space through cross-functional synergy."

→ **WORD SALAD**

*Opens fast and gets a laugh. Proves it's live before anything slow happens.*

---

**2. The true one** — proves it isn't just a hater

> "Usain Bolt ran the hundred metres in 9.58 seconds."

→ **NO CAP**

*Important that this comes before the dunk. If the first two are both red,
it looks like a machine that says no.*

---

**3. The dunk** — end here

> "And Notion costs eight dollars per seat per month."

→ **ABSOLUTE CAP** · *actually: $20 per seat per month* · notion.com

*The correction is the payoff. It doesn't just say you're wrong, it tells you
what's right and shows you the page.*

---

## Saying it out loud

> "We're going to revolutionise the space through cross-functional synergy.
> Usain Bolt ran the hundred metres in 9.58 seconds.
> And Notion costs eight dollars per seat per month."

That's about 12 seconds of speech and 30 seconds of video once the verdicts
land.

## Don't

- Don't read it fast. Pauses are what let it close a sentence.
- Don't say all three then wait. Say one, let it land, say the next.
- Don't add a fourth. Three is the whole story: nonsense, true, false.
